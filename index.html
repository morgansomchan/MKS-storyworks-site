How it works

Four phases, running on a monthly loop.

  1. 01

    Research

    I learn your business, study your competitors and find where your actual advantage is. Out of that comes the strategy, the brand revamp, and every link and listing fixed.

  2. 02

    Produce

    Shoot it, film it, edit, caption, post. Everything built on the pillars and the voice we settled in research, so nothing goes up that sounds like someone else.

  3. 03

    Report

    End of every month, every channel, the numbers organised and explained in plain language rather than handed to you raw.

  4. 04

    Iterate

    What worked, what didn't. We double down on what did, and that decides next month's plan. Then it runs again.

Every month the loop runs again, and every month it starts from better information than the last one.

Across the whole ecosystem: Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile, LINE and your website.

Who I am

I'm Morgan.

I grew up in Ithaca. I studied Media and Business at NYU's New York and Shanghai campuses, with a focus in marketing, and spent my gap year in Bangkok.

In Bangkok I kept finding cafés and restaurants with real personality. Forty years of history, a core value, an owner who could talk for an hour about one dish. Then I'd look them up online and find none of it there. Generic photos, and captions that could have belonged to any restaurant in the city.

So I started helping them share that passion online. Not by posting more, but by uncovering the story that was already there and building strategy and systems around it.

Based in Ithaca, New York. Clients wherever they are.
