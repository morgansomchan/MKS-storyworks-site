Case Study

What it looks like in practice.

One restaurant, eighteen months, and the transformation.

Le Dalat/Bangkok, Thailand

Le Dalat has served the most authentic Vietnamese Fine-dining experience in Bangkok for over 40 years. The same family, same recipes being passed down and preserved. The problem was they had trouble attracting new customers so they tried posting on instagram, facebook, & LINE but it wasn't working and took too much time.

So I went in and told them to stop posting. We took a step back and I dove deep into their unique operational story, what their competitors are doing and how they could differentiate themselves. Their unique story gave new customers a reason to choose this restaurant over every other Vietnamese restaurant in Bangkok.

Once they had momentum, I built a loyalty program from scratch to enhance the customer experience. Then I looked at their finances and saw the reservation platform was bleeding them = $180+ a month in per-head fees. I built them a fully custom website with reservations built in. Fixed cost. Gave them full control and room to actually grow.

Before you book

Questions you're probably having.

What does this cost?

It depends on what you need. one platform or three, whether production is in scope, whether there's a website in it. I'll give you an accurate number on the call, not a range designed to get you on one.

You're one person. What happens when you're sick, or on a plane?

I have systems for this. The calendar is built weeks ahead, so nothing stops because I'm on a plane or out for a day. Anything live like a shoot, a campaign launch is scheduled around dates I give you in advance.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

At an agency you meet the person who sells the work, and then you work with the person who didn't. Big team, high prices, and you're one name on their long list of clients.

I take five clients at a time. That's the rule. And it's deliberate because it's the only way the quality holds across all of them. You deal with me from the first conversation to the monthly report. If I bring someone in on a piece of it, I'll tell you who and why.

We don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

No — it's usually the first thing we fix. I direct the shoot either way: the brief, the shot list, what every frame is for. When I'm local I shoot it myself. When I'm not, I brief a production partner near you and direct from there. The direction is mine regardless.

How long before this shows up in the numbers?

Google Business Profile moves fastest; often a few weeks. Social is a longer build; think in quarters. Anyone promising faster than that on organic is guessing.

You're in New York and my restaurant isn't.

Le Dalat is in Bangkok, and I've never needed to be in the room for the strategy, the writing, or the reporting. The only part that needs someone physically present is camera work.

What do you need from me every month?

Depends how hands-on you want to be. Some clients want to be in the day to day. Usually we meet monthly or every other week and build things together. Others may want to hand it over and see it work; I deliver, and we meet when it works best for you. Both are fine. What you won't have to do is chase me for an update.

Do you only work with restaurants?

Restaurants and lifestyle brands. If you're outside that, I'll see if we can still work together but if not I'll tell you on the call rather than force you into a retainer.
